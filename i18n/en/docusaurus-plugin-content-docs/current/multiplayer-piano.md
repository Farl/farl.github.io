## About the project {#作品介紹}

A multiplayer piano platform where everyone can play piano together in real time. Each player can choose their own piano tone and play simultaneously with others.

## Features {#特點}

- Multiplayer real-time piano playing
- Customizable piano tones for each player
- Online multiplayer connectivity
