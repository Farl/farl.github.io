## From a pattern to a three-dimensional model {#從圖紙到立體造型}

Paper ox patterns in different shapes and colors, including a version you can customize.

The complete patterns from the original website have been preserved. Choose a variant, preview the sheets, and download the original files to print and assemble.

## Make one yourself {#動手製作}

Choose a pattern below and download the original file for printing. Cut along the outline, then fold and assemble the pieces. If an assembly video is available, watch it before you begin.
