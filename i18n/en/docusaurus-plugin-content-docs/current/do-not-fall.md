## My contribution {#參與紀錄}

I contributed to **Do Not Fall — Run For Your Drink** as **Creative Lead** in 2013.

Platforms: PlayStation 3, Arcade.

This project is part of my team production experience. The game images show work made together by the team.

## A design that began with multiplayer {#從多人對戰開始的設計}

Every floor tile disappears after it is stepped on, so each move changes the choices that follow. The original concept centered on four-player matches: use pushing, routes and items to make opponents fall while keeping your own footing. That core mechanic also grew into single-player levels, combining jumps, dashes, obstacles and traps into an adventure that asks for both quick reactions and judgment.

[Read my introduction on PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2013/07/22/do-not-fall-out-on-psn-tomorrow/).
