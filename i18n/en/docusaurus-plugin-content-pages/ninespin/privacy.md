# Ninespin Privacy Policy

[正體中文](pathname:///ninespin/privacy)

Effective date: 2026-10-26

This policy explains how Ninespin (九宮轉), a puzzle game by Farl Lee ("I", "me") for iOS, Android and the web, handles your data.

## What I don't collect

Ninespin has no accounts. It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or other information that directly identifies you.

## Local save data

Your level progress and settings are stored only on your device (in the app's storage or the browser's local storage) and are not uploaded to my servers. Deleting the app or clearing browser data removes them.

## Anonymous analytics (PostHog)

To improve the game, the app sends anonymous gameplay events through [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy), such as levels started and completed, moves used and time taken. These events carry only a random ID created at install time, which is not linked to your identity, your advertising ID or any account.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The iOS and Android versions show ads between stages through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may use:

- your device's advertising ID (the Android advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt)
- device information (such as model, operating system, language and approximate location, e.g. country derived from IP address)
- ad interaction and usage data

to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent at launch through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP); you can consent or manage options. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can also reset your advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device settings.

## Children

Ninespin is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date above.
